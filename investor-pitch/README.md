# Investor Pitch Helper

A phone-friendly web app for the BBSI Grade 12 Entrepreneurship Third Quarter Performance
Task, **Investor Proposal: Seed Funding for a Kaytikling Venture**. It follows the task's 15 steps.

| Screen | What the app does |
|---|---|
| Plan | Team members and the four roles (one member may hold two), key dates with countdown, progress through the 15 steps, the 50-point rubric, copy all as text |
| A · Find a real need | Interview log (at least 10) with a tally of the problems mentioned, observations, fishbone diagram, problem tree, and opportunity screening on the six criteria (1–5 each) with ranking |
| B · Build the product | Value proposition sentence builder, target market, prototype notes, testing log (at least 5) with average willingness to pay, and changes made |
| C · Plan the business | Pitch outline in the required order with an estimated speaking time, plan sections, organizational chart from the roles, the 4Ms and 7Ps, compliance checklist (business name, barangay clearance, mayor's permit, BIR), and social responsibility |
| ₱ · Financial plan | Supplier canvass (two prices per item, lower one used), start-up cost, FC and VC, unit cost, selling price with markup and rounding, break-even point with chart, three-month projected income statement, ROI with working capital, and a lower-sales what-if |
| D · Proposal and pitch | The investor ask and what it buys, proposal section checklist in the required order, 10-slide deck plan, 5-minute pitch and 3-minute Q&A timers, likely investor questions with the team's own numbers, feedback and Quarter 4 improvements |

## Teacher Check

Open **Plan → Open Teacher Check** (or add `#teacher` to the link). It asks for the teacher PIN first; only a SHA-256 hash of the PIN is stored in the code (`TC_HASH`). Type in a team's start-up cost, FC, VC, units, markup (or price), and monthly units to get every computation.

Equations are typeset with MathJax (local copy in `../lib/mathjax`). Everything is saved in the phone's browser. Works offline after the first visit.

Link for students (GitHub Pages): `https://compact-criterion.github.io/auto-manae/investor-pitch/`
