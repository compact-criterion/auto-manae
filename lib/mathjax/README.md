# MathJax 3.2.2 (tex-svg bundle)

A local copy of `es5/tex-svg.js` from the `mathjax@3.2.2` npm package, used by all three
performance task apps to typeset their equations and step-by-step solutions in LaTeX.
Keeping it here means the apps do not depend on an outside website and keep working offline.
